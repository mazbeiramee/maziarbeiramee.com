Venture Models

What young companies might be worth

Independent practice models I built using only public information, each asking what a startup might be worth today and what an investment in it could return.

Every input in the models is tagged VERIFIED (from a cited public source) or ASSUMPTION (my own estimate). This is practice analysis, not investment advice.

Defense testing01

Valkryn

Valkryn runs independent “Battle Lab” tests of military drones, ground robots, uncrewed boats and counter-drone systems, and it does not build the systems it tests.

Round
$15M seed
Lead
a16z American Dynamism
Date
Nov 17, 2026

How the model works

Valkryn hasn’t said what it’s worth, so I estimated its value after this round at about $75 million (somewhere between $60 million and $100 million), based on how much of a company investors usually buy in a round this size and on what similar startups were valued at. Then I asked what a $1 million check at that price could turn into, after later funding rounds shrink its share. A bad outcome returns about 0.2x the money, a normal one about 5.6x and a great one about 22.5x, or about 4.3x on average once I weight each outcome by how likely I think it is. The valuation, exit values and odds are my estimates, because none of them are public.
