Pulseline is a company I founded that built AI voice agents for HVAC businesses. The agents handled inbound calls, appointment booking, lead qualification, and customer follow-up, the work that tends to slip when a small team is out on jobs.

Before building, I conducted more than 20 customer discovery interviews with HVAC operators to understand where their workflows break down. Those conversations shaped pricing, the integrations we prioritized, and the product requirements.

I also analyzed more than 20 competitors across AI voice, call handling, and field-service software to understand pricing, positioning, and gaps ahead of a pilot launch.
