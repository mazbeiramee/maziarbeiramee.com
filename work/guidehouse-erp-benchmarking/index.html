State ERP modernization at Guidehouse

I interned at Guidehouse, where I benchmarked state ERP modernization programs and mapped the systems, reports, and processes of a statewide program serving about 5,000 users.

Focus

I interned at Guidehouse in the summer of 2026 on the Communities, Energy and Infrastructure team in Arlington, Virginia.

I conducted comparative benchmarking of state ERP modernization programs, looking at cost, implementation timelines, governance, procurement, ROI, and lessons learned.

I analyzed enterprise architecture across GL, AP, AR, and cross-functional workstreams, reviewing more than 320 systems, 760 reports, 4,200 business processes, and 60 interfaces to identify dependencies.

For Guidehouse's CBI Program Delivery practice, I built a centralized content repository, a modular pitch deck, a capability two-pager, and a visual library, and presented them to stakeholders.

Client names are omitted for confidentiality.
